## Songs and quotations

Many poems became songs, and isolated lines often travel farther than the
poems from which they came. Quotations are efficient travellers, though they
rarely carry all their luggage.

## Political memory

Heine's writing has repeatedly returned to debates about censorship, exile,
nationalism, and belonging. The circumstances change; the temptation to make
easy certainties does not.

### A name for a small generator

The name Heine belongs with Hugo and Zola in a literary naming tradition among
static-site generators. It is a reference, not a claim to their gifts.

## Read the work

Biographical notes are useful doors. The poems and prose are the rooms beyond
them.
