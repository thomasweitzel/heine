## Poetry

Heine's lyric poetry can sound direct, musical, and deceptively light. Its
clarity often leaves room for an afterthought that unsettles the first reading.

### Irony is part of the sentence

In Heine, irony is not merely decoration. It changes what a sentence permits
itself to mean, especially when a grand declaration is asking to be believed.

## Prose

His essays, criticism, and political writing move easily between observation,
argument, and satire. They also show why literary form and public life are not
as separate as respectable institutions sometimes prefer.

## A useful disagreement

Readers need not agree with every judgment to recognize the force of the
prose. A writer who makes disagreement more precise has already done useful
work.
